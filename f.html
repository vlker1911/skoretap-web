Stáhnout aplikaci Skoretap pro Android

Na iPhonu zatím zapisovat nejde. Ať zapisuje někdo z hráčů s Androidem.

Get the Skoretap app for Android

For now, federation matches can be scored only in the Android app. Install it, sign in, then scan the QR code on the court again — the app opens the match.

Scoring on iPhone is not available yet. Let a player with an Android phone keep score.
